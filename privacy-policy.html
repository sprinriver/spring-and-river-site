Privacy Policy

How Sprinriver Technology Private Limited handles information collected through springandriver.com.

Overview

This Privacy Policy describes the types of information collected and recorded by Spring & River (Sprinriver Technology Private Limited) through this website, and how that information is used. If you have questions about this policy, contact us at info@springandriver.com.

This policy applies only to information collected through this website. It does not apply to information collected offline, or through channels other than this website.

Information we collect

When you contact us through this website, you choose what to send us. The enquiry form on our contact page composes a message in your own email client — it does not transmit your details to this website or to any third-party form service. The information we then hold is whatever you include in the email you choose to send: typically your name, company, email address and a description of your enquiry.

Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically record standard server log information, which can include IP address, browser type, referring pages, and the date and time of requests. This information is not linked to personally identifiable information and is used for aggregate analysis of site performance and usage.

How we use information

Information you send us is used to respond to your enquiry, to provide and improve our services, to communicate with you about your enquiry or engagement, and to meet legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information.

Confidentiality of client information

Our clients hold confidential information, intellectual property rights in software and business processes, and commercially sensitive information about operations, performance and stakeholders. We maintain strict confidentiality in these matters and work under non-disclosure agreements. Client identities and project details are not published on this website.

Third-party services

This website loads web fonts from Google Fonts, which may involve a request to Google's servers when you view a page. This site does not run advertising networks, and does not embed third-party tracking or analytics scripts.

External sites linked from here have their own privacy policies. We have no control over, and accept no responsibility for, the content or practices of those sites.

Data retention

Correspondence is retained for as long as needed to respond to your enquiry and to meet our legal and contractual obligations, after which it is deleted or anonymised.

Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you, to request correction or deletion of it, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. To exercise any of these rights, contact info@springandriver.com.

Children

This website is a business-to-business site and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes take effect when the revised policy is posted on this page.
