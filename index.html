Services

Five competencies that stack

Most clients start with one and grow into the rest. Each is delivered as a working system with lineage, monitoring and handover — not a slide deck.

Data Unification

Converge plant historians, quality systems and enterprise records onto one timeseries spine that every downstream model can rely on.

Data Engineering

Medallion architectures, real-time streaming and connectors to LIMS, ELN, MES, Historian and D365 — with lineage and quality enforced end to end.

Business Intelligence

Power BI dashboards and custom Plotly toolkits: shop-floor visualisation and executive KPI rollups fed from the same conformed data.

AI & Machine Learning

Predictive models, timeseries forecasting, computer vision and statistical analysis — from XGBoost on production lines to Nextflow pipelines for genomics.

Agentic AI

MCP servers and multi-agent orchestration with three-layer, defence-in-depth allowlisting and a full audit trail. Production-grade — already shipped.
