Six industries, one framework

We have applied our AI Solutions Framework across metals, thermal processing, composites, pharma, automotive and renewable biomass. The use cases below are the ones that recur — described generically, because client work stays under NDA.

Metal Manufacturing Heat Furnace Sheet Molding Pharma Automobile Renewable Biomass

Metal Manufacturing

Iron and steelmaking generates enormous volumes of sensor data against comparatively sparse quality outcomes. The value is in relating the two — reliably enough to move setpoints.

MM-01

Improving hot metal production

Relationships are built between historical machine sensor data and output quality from factory registers and quality audit reports. Compliant ranges per parameter are established, and furnace process control equipment is held within them.

Outcome — consistent hot metal quality, improved productivity
MM-02

Alloy & scrap mix optimisation

Charge and alloy additions are optimised against the target grade using models trained on heat chemistry, scrap composition and final analysis — meeting specification with the lowest-cost chemistry rather than the safest overshoot.

Outcome — reduced ferroalloy cost per tonne
MM-03

Caster & rolling defect detection

Surface inspection imagery combined with process conditions identifies defect signatures as they emerge, and traces them back to the casting or rolling parameters that produced them.

Outcome — less downgraded product and rework
MM-04

Yield recovery across the chain

Losses are attributed across melt, cast, roll and finish rather than reported as a single plant number, so improvement effort is directed at the stage actually responsible.

Outcome — measurable yield improvement

Heat Furnace Manufacturing

Thermal processing is where energy cost and product quality are decided simultaneously. Small, sustained improvements in combustion control compound quickly.

HF-01

Optimising fuel consumption

Operating data for boilers and turbines — fuel flow, water flow, steam temperature, heat generated, combustion gas, exhaust composition — captured at minute resolution over a full year is used to model optimal fuel and water rates per asset.

Outcome — significant fuel savings across boilers and turbines
HF-02

Asset combination scheduling

With per-asset models in place, a further model recommends which combination of gas and steam turbines, all of differing capacities, should run to meet daily, weekly and monthly power requirements.

Outcome — demand met at lowest total fuel cost
HF-03

Thermal profile & soak control

Zone temperatures and residence times are tuned against metallurgical or curing targets, reducing both under-soak rejects and the energy wasted on unnecessary over-soak.

Outcome — tighter property distribution, lower energy per tonne
HF-04

Refractory life prediction

Thermal cycling history, campaign duration and inspection findings are modelled to forecast refractory wear, so relines are scheduled against condition rather than a fixed calendar.

Outcome — planned relines, avoided unplanned outages

Sheet Molding Manufacturing

Composites are chosen for performance at low weight — which only holds if the reinforcement is uniform. Uniformity is a vision problem before it is a process problem.

SM-01

Analysing microstructures

Microscope images of composite cross sections are annotated for a representative sample; a deep learning model learns to identify individual fibres by texture, colour, size and shape, then scores mix consistency across the full section.

Outcome — non-uniform material segregated early
SM-02

Constituent ratio verification

The model numerically expresses the percentages of carbon and thermoplastic present, mapped against set standards. Where any metric falls below tolerance, the problem is identified and remedial action taken.

Outcome — quality verified per part, not per batch sample
SM-03

Cure cycle optimisation

Press temperature, pressure and dwell are related to finished mechanical properties, identifying the shortest cure that still meets specification across multi-fibre systems.

Outcome — shorter cycle time at equal quality
SM-04

Scrap and rework reduction

Charge weight, flow behaviour and mould conditions are analysed against scrap events to identify the controllable drivers, rather than attributing loss to material variability by default.

Outcome — lower scrap rate on high-value material

Pharma & Life Sciences

Regulated environments raise the bar on reproducibility and audit. Every pipeline we build here is expected to justify its output, not merely produce it.

PH-01

Batch record analytics

Electronic batch records, LIMS results and equipment data are unified so that batch-to-batch variation can be examined across the full manufacturing history rather than investigated one deviation at a time.

Outcome — faster investigations, fewer repeat deviations
PH-02

Deviation & OOS prediction

Models trained on in-process parameters flag batches trending toward an out-of-specification result while intervention is still possible, instead of confirming the loss at final release testing.

Outcome — intervention before batch value is lost
PH-03

Reproducible genomics pipelines

Nextflow pipelines for sequencing and bioinformatics workloads, containerised and version-pinned so that a result obtained today can be regenerated identically months later.

Outcome — reproducibility that survives audit
PH-04

LIMS & ELN integration

Laboratory systems are connected as governed sources rather than exported by hand, giving analytics and AI access to instrument and assay data under the same controls as the rest of the estate.

Outcome — lab data usable without manual handling

Automobile

High volume and deep supply chains mean a defect caught one station later is dramatically more expensive. Detection has to be immediate and traceable.

AU-01

Weld & joint quality inspection

Computer vision on robotic welding stations assesses joint quality in line, replacing sampled manual inspection with continuous assessment tied back to the cell and the parameters in use.

Outcome — defects caught at the station that made them
AU-02

Paint shop defect analytics

Booth conditions, film thickness and defect imagery are related to identify the environmental and process drivers behind recurring finish faults, which are rarely attributable to one variable alone.

Outcome — reduced repaint and polish load
AU-03

Supplier quality analytics

Incoming component data is analysed against downstream assembly and test outcomes, quantifying which supplier lots actually correlate with line stoppages and field issues.

Outcome — evidence-based supplier conversations
AU-04

Warranty pattern mining

Warranty claims, free-text technician notes and build records are mined together with NLP to surface emerging failure patterns earlier than claim volume alone would reveal them.

Outcome — earlier containment of field issues

Renewable Biomass Manufacturing

Feedstock is inherently variable, which makes fixed recipes wasteful. Models that adapt to what actually arrived at the gate recover most of that loss.

RB-01

Feedstock variability compensation

Incoming species, moisture and particle size are characterised and process setpoints adjusted per lot, rather than running a single conservative recipe that suits the worst-case input.

Outcome — stable product from variable input
RB-02

Torrefaction & pyrolysis yield

Reactor temperature, residence time and feed rate are optimised against energy density and mass yield, balancing the two objectives explicitly instead of by operator preference.

Outcome — higher yield at target energy density
RB-03

Moisture & drying control

Dryer performance is modelled against ambient conditions and feed characteristics to hold outlet moisture in band while avoiding the substantial energy cost of over-drying.

Outcome — reduced drying energy per tonne
RB-04

Emissions & carbon accounting

Process and utility data are unified into auditable emissions and carbon-intensity reporting, tied to the same records used for production accounting so the two cannot diverge.

Outcome — defensible sustainability reporting
