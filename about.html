We resolve business problems, not just deliver IT applications

Sprinriver Technology Private Limited trades as Spring & River. We provide Business Intelligence, Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning technology solutions and services to customers across the USA, Canada, the UK, Sweden, the UAE and India.

We are faster

We have built ready-to-use frameworks for a large number of AI techniques. We select the one most suited, modify as required, and are ready for testing sooner.

We are cost effective

Since we spend less time, we spend less money. We save through video conferencing and offshore development — even initial implementation can be remotely managed.

We collaborate

We precisely define the problem, define the risks and conduct a feasibility analysis. Oversight of development is the responsibility of the collaborative team.

We respect your rights

You have IPR in software and business processes, and secrets about operations, performance and stakeholders. We maintain strict confidentiality in these matters.

Our team

Our development team is well versed in both traditional software and AI solution techniques. We also hold a bank of people from multiple industries, which is what gives us domain depth alongside the engineering.
