Consult. Build. Ship.

Three phases, in order. Consulting comes first so nobody spends development budget on a problem that was never properly stated — or one the data cannot answer.

01The Consulting Phase

Discovery

We are consultants. We listen, and we take both the success stories and the pain points seriously before proposing anything.

  • Setting the stage and understanding the business process
  • Articulating the problem statement and setting out deliverables
  • Performing feasibility analysis, which may involve a few proofs of concept on test bays
  • Defining and apprising the customer of the risks involved
02The Development Phase

Experiment & Execute

We now have a problem worth solving and a proposed methodology. This phase establishes whether the data supports it, and which technique performs best on your process rather than in general.

  • Checking information flow, data sources, formats and data availability
  • Building the data flow diagram and preparing data to create the model
  • Model design — building test data sets
  • Model design — applying candidate AI techniques
  • Selecting the model giving the best results
03The Implementation Phase

Implement

We now have actionable information that resolves the problem identified in consulting. What remains is getting it into daily operational use and keeping it there.

  • Preparing infrastructure
  • Implementing the solution into the operating environment
  • Trial runs and fine tuning against live conditions
  • Handover, monitoring and support
What you can expect

Ground rules we hold to

Feasibility before commitment

We would rather tell you early that the available data cannot support the question than bill for a build that quietly underdelivers. The risks are named in writing during discovery.

Your data stays yours

Customers hold confidential information, IPR in software and business processes, and secrets about operations, performance and stakeholders. We maintain strict confidentiality across all of it.

The plant team stays in charge

Models recommend; engineers and operators decide. We build systems that make expert reasoning available on every shift, not systems that automate decisions away from the people accountable for them.

Delivered, not demonstrated

A model that only runs on a laptop is not finished. Deliverables include pipelines, monitoring, documentation and the handover needed for your team to own it.

Before you ask

Questions we hear often

Our data is messy and spread across a dozen systems. Do we need to fix that first?

No — that situation is where most engagements begin. Data unification is the first stage of our framework: raw signals are landed at full fidelity, then conformed and quality-checked before any model sees them. Cleaning your data in advance would mean doing part of the project before the project.

How do you handle confidentiality and intellectual property?

An NDA is in place before process details or data change hands. Your IPR in software and business processes, and your operational and commercial secrets, remain yours — and client names are never published on this site, which is why our use cases are described generically.

Do we have to commit to a large build up front?

No. The consulting phase exists precisely so you do not: discovery ends with a written problem statement, a feasibility analysis — sometimes including proofs of concept on test bays — and the risks named plainly. Development is only signed off after that.

Will this replace the dashboards and ERP we already use?

We build on what you run rather than replacing it. Connectors cover LIMS, ELN, MES, plant historians and Dynamics 365, and reporting is delivered in Power BI or custom toolkits fed from the same conformed data your existing systems trust.

Bring us a process problem, not a data project.

Tell us where the losses are — yield, energy, quality, downtime — and we will scope a feasibility study on your own data before anyone commits to a build.
