Texplicit

A B2B, AI-driven knowledge management solution — built for organisations whose expertise is scattered across documents nobody has time to read.

What it does

Combine external research with your own internal knowledge

Texplicit lets a user combine knowledge collected from videos, podcasts and research papers on the web with the organisation's own internal documents — policy manuals, standard operating procedures, quality methods, legal agreements and more.

It searches and locates the relevant parts of any document at the click of a mouse, using NLP and other AI tools to digest and curate the information, and answers specific user queries against it.

Summaries and highlights obtained through Texplicit can be downloaded in Word or PowerPoint format for future use.

Visit texplicit.com → Request a walkthrough

Curated, not just retrieved

NLP digests and curates source material rather than returning a list of links, so the answer arrives with the supporting passages attached.

Internal and external sources together

Organisational documents sit alongside web research in one searchable body of knowledge, which is where most tools force a choice.

Exports into real deliverables

Summaries and highlights download as Word or PowerPoint, so the output feeds the document someone actually has to produce.
